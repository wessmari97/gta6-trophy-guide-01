Road to the platinum

Five phases, in the order that works. The story earns 14 trophies on its own; the rest come from stranger strands, activities, collectibles and the long grinds.

  1. Phase 1 — Play the story

    Chapters 1 to 4, the named missions and the finale. 14 trophies, none of them missable except the two choice moments — do those with care, or replay the mission afterwards.

  2. Phase 2 — Stranger strands

    Finish the mission chains for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, plus Bret's hunt. Eight finished strands earn the Strangers & Freaks silver.

  3. Phase 3 — Activities

    Try every activity once, then chase the wins: 20 different races, gold medals at the shooting range, drone races at every location, and Cal's fishing challenge.

  4. Phase 4 — Collectibles and the map

    Meditation spots, card sets, 100 vehicles, every garage, and the whole of Leonida explored. Sweep region by region so nothing is left behind.

  5. Phase 5 — Grinds and 100%

    250 stunts, 100 WAiNK It locations, $250,000 of Marked Bills laundered, $100,000 spent — then 100% completion, and the platinum pops.

⚠ 10 trophies are missable: single moments in missions or the open world. They are flagged below — do them while the chance is open, or replay the mission from the pause menu.
