Roadmap to the platinum

  1. 1 · Play the story. Chapters 1–4 and the finale earn 14 trophies on their own, including the story gold. Do the missable moments when they appear — they are flagged below.
  2. 2 · Stranger strands. Finish the mission chains for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, plus three more, for the Strangers & Freaks silver.
  3. 3 · Activities. Races, fishing, the shooting range, drone races, the arcade — one of each, then golds and wins where the guide asks.
  4. 4 · Collectibles & grinds. Cards, meditation spots, vehicles, garages, WAiNK spots, stunts and challenges. Sweep the map region by region and tick them off here.
  5. 5 · 100% completion. Check the in-game stats for whatever is left, then A Long Road and the platinum pop together.
